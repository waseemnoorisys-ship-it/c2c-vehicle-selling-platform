# C2C Vehicle Selling Platform

> C2C Motors is a peer-to-peer automotive marketplace connecting vehicle buyers and private sellers directly across Europe. The platform provides bank-grade Escrow payment protection, certified 200-point mechanical inspections, real-time messaging, and intelligent vehicle valuation tools.

## Core Capabilities & Features

- **Direct Peer-to-Peer Car Trading**: Buy and sell pre-owned and luxury vehicles directly without dealership markups.
- **Escrow Vault Protection**: Payments are locked in regulated escrow accounts until the buyer physically inspects and approves handover.
- **Certified 200-Point Mechanical Inspection**: Nationwide ASE-certified technicians conduct mechanical, diagnostic, and bodywork inspections prior to listing publication.
- **Auto Loan & Financing Calculator**: Real-time auto loan payment estimator with custom vehicle price, down payment, interest rate, and term options.
- **Vehicle Valuation Estimator**: Data-driven market pricing tool providing instant private party price ranges and trade-in comparisons based on live sales records.
- **Direct Buyer-Seller Chat**: Embedded real-time messaging and negotiation platform with pre-funded binding offer capabilities.
